Cookie Policy

Last updated: February 19, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Logsome ("we", "our", or "us"), operated by Samagra Tech, uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit logsome.app.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website when you visit it. They help websites remember information about your visit to make it work correctly and improve your experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

Logsome uses a minimal set of cookies. We are committed to only using cookies that are strictly necessary to operate the Service. We do not use cookies for advertising, behavioural tracking, or analytics.

Essential Cookies

These cookies are required for the Service to function. Without them, you would not be able to log in or use the app.

What We Do Not Use

We do not use:

  • Analytics cookies (e.g., Google Analytics)
  • Advertising or tracking cookies
  • Social media cookies
  • Performance monitoring cookies from third parties

3. Third-Party Cookies

We rely on a small number of third-party services that may set their own cookies. These are limited to essential infrastructure (e.g., our cloud hosting provider). We do not permit third parties to use cookies for advertising or tracking on our behalf.

4. Managing Cookies

Since we only use essential cookies, disabling them will affect the core functionality of Logsome — specifically, you will not be able to stay logged in.

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Please note that restricting essential cookies may prevent you from using the Service.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected here with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to check back periodically.
